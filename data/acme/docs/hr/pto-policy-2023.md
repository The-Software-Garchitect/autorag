Superseded on December 31, 2024. This page is kept so old links do not 404. Do not use it for a current balance. The questions people still paste into Slack are answered below with the 2023 numbers.

## Annual allotment

During the first year of service, employees receive 12 PTO days per year.

After 1 year of service, employees receive 12 PTO days per year.

After 3 years of service, employees receive 16 PTO days per year.

After 5 years of service, employees receive 20 PTO days per year.

## Carryover and accrual

The maximum PTO carryover is 0 days.

## Questions people still ask

Q: How many PTO days after 3 years? A: 16 PTO days.

Q: What is the maximum PTO carryover? A: 0 days.

Q: How many days a week did office-assigned employees work on site under this era? See the 2023 remote page, not this one.
