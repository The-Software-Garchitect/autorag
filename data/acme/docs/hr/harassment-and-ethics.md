You can report to your manager, to People, or straight to the ethics line. You do not have to have a perfect writeup to start.

## Reporting

Report harassment to People or to ethics@acme.example.

The ethics hotline is 1-800-555-0199.

## Retaliation

Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited.
