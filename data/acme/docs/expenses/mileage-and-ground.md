Drive your own car only when it is the sensible way to the airport or a customer site. Log miles from the office or from home, whichever is shorter.

## Mileage

The mileage reimbursement rate is $0.70 per mile.

## Rideshare

Rideshare trips require a receipt when the fare is over $25.
