Use these city rates instead of the standard travel meal caps when you are staying overnight. Lodging is a nightly cap before tax.

## City rates

| City | Lodging per diem | Meals and incidental expenses per diem | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Austin | $180 | $64 | The Austin cap assumes a downtown hotel within walking distance of the headquarters. |
| Denver | $190 | $69 | Denver lodging should be booked near Union Station when the meeting is downtown. |
| New York | $280 | $79 | New York lodging fills early, so the travel desk holds a backup hotel. |
| San Francisco | $270 | $79 | San Francisco lodging near the ferry building is usually at the cap. |
| London | $310 | $90 | London lodging is booked in zone 1 only when the client site is there. |
| Chicago | $220 | $74 | Chicago lodging per diem covers a downtown hotel near the river. |

Read the row that matches the plan or the city. Do not average across rows, and do not use a superseded rate sheet.
